# Agent prompts

Copy one prompt into your agent. Replace `<workspace>` with your private folder’s absolute path.

## Setup prompt

Read `<workspace>/AGENTS.md` and `skills/setup-job-search/SKILL.md`. Help me set up a private job-search and interview-preparation workspace. Check which files and tools you can actually access. Ask me a small first batch of questions about target roles, location, constraints, resume source, storage preference and action boundaries. Do not assume any candidate facts or that connectors are installed.

Offer local Markdown, Obsidian, or Notion. Tell me what I need to install/connect for my chosen option, then configure the records and navigation using the available tools. Keep one canonical store and document it in the canonical-storage section of INSTRUCTIONS.md. If you cannot write files, prepare the files for me and state the limit clearly. Persist my confirmed answers, unresolved facts and current task. Default to shortlist and review together. Finish by validating the workspace and giving me one useful next action.


## Discover jobs

Read AGENTS.md and the job-search skill in my workspace. Review my current preferences, tracker, company queue and recent search log. Research a small batch of due or new companies, verify exact official roles and active application routes, record coverage limits and give me a concise evidence-backed shortlist. Do not apply or contact anyone.


## Prepare for an interview

Read AGENTS.md and the interview-preparation skill. I will identify the target role and provide any invitation details. Review the role and my career evidence, ask for essential missing format details, then prepare and preflight one exercise. Ask only its opening question. Preserve my attempt, record assistance and feedback, and keep hypothetical answers separate from career facts.


## Reconcile application updates

Read AGENTS.md and the applications skill. Reconcile the updates I supply with exact roles and dated evidence. If I explicitly ask you to inspect email, use read-only access and record scope and ambiguities. Update canonical records and the current handoff. Do not send messages or alter my mailbox.


## Review the search

Read my current workspace and the setup-job-search skill (periodic review). Find due actions, stale verifications, unresolved ownership, interview priorities and repeated research. Propose three next actions. Do not contact anyone or create recurring automation.


## Resume in a new chat

Open my workspace at <absolute-workspace-path>. Read AGENTS.md, INSTRUCTIONS.md and CONTEXT.md, then profile/CANDIDATE.md including its open questions. Inspect the canonical records needed for the current task. Tell me the active task and next action, then continue within existing authorization. Save changes and a concise handoff during the session.
