This is the discovery/coverage index, not an application ledger. A partial title review does not establish full company or market coverage. Preserve user deferrals and referral-only boundaries.

Preserve the first-discovered date on later checks. Record the actual recheck date or a concrete trigger, plus its reason. Choose cadence from posting churn, prior results and the user’s priorities rather than assuming a universal interval. A due date is a planning reminder, not a scheduled background job.
