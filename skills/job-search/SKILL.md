# Job Search

Read preferences, candidate evidence, tracker, company queue, recent search log and relevant role notes. Select a bounded batch with a reason for each new check. Respect deferred/declined and referral-only decisions. Use the user's title families and geographic constraints.

Inventory relevant official-board titles and requisitions, including pagination and filters. Compare against tracked IDs and aliases. Verify each promising exact official posting, description, location eligibility and active Apply route on the current search date. Search snippets and aggregators are discovery aids only. If a dynamic board cannot be verified, record the limit and retry trigger instead of claiming no roles exist.

Research business context and actual responsibilities. Compare candidate evidence, level, compensation basis, work model and travel. Distinguish source facts from assessment. Silence on travel is unknown. Avoid padded shortlists and duplicates. Update company coverage, search log, exact role notes and tracker. Present fit, caveats, links, verification date and next decision. Discovery does not authorize applying or contacting people.
