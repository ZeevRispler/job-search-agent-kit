# Job Search

Read preferences, candidate evidence, tracker, company queue, recent search log and relevant role notes. Select a bounded batch with a reason for each new check. Record each company’s first-discovered date/source, last-checked date, outcome and next recheck date or explicit trigger in the company queue. For an exact role, preserve its first-discovered date/source and verification history in its role note. Do not reset discovery dates on later checks. Skip recently checked companies unless their recheck is due, a trigger fires or the user requests them. Respect deferred/declined and referral-only decisions. Use the user's title families and geographic constraints.

Inventory relevant official-board titles and requisitions, including pagination and filters. Compare against tracked IDs and aliases. Verify each promising exact official posting, description, location eligibility and active Apply route on the current search date. Reverify immediately before recommending a saved role as live and again before application preparation. A saved URL or previous live check does not establish that it is still open. If the exact page is missing, closed, redirects to a generic board or fails to expose verifiable content and an application route, mark availability closed or unverified and exclude it from the active shortlist. Preserve any ongoing application process separately. Search snippets and aggregators are discovery aids only. If a dynamic board cannot be verified, record the limit and retry trigger instead of claiming no roles exist.

Research business context and actual responsibilities. Compare candidate evidence, level, compensation basis, work model and travel. Distinguish source facts from assessment. Silence on travel is unknown. Avoid padded shortlists and duplicates. Update company coverage, search log, exact role notes and tracker. Present fit, caveats, links, verification date and next decision. Discovery does not authorize applying or contacting people.
