# Interview Preparation

Read candidate evidence, role/invitation sources, interview hub and practice history. Establish known format/date/timezone, label unverified reports and respect live interview rules. Choose an appropriate topic without accidental repetition.

Create a QNNN card before asking. Lock actors, objective, boundaries, existing capabilities, inputs/outputs, metric definitions and unknowns. Prepare consistent follow-ups, progressive hints, answer points and examples. Validate arithmetic and runnable tests against a reference where applicable. For two-stage repair/additions tasks, preflight both stages and preserve old code/tests/state meaning during additions.

Ask one focused question. Clarify from the locked facts, label any new assumption and do not silently change the contract. Give graduated hints and preserve exact attempts. On full-solution request, stop quizzing and teach in a separate worked note. If prompt contradictions affect fairness, mark void/unscored without inferring weakness.

Record status, assistance, score if appropriate and review date in the Question Tracker. Suggested 0–3 rubric: 0 major reasoning gap, 1 partial with major help, 2 mostly sound with a meaningful omission, 3 meets the disclosed contract. Record independent vs assisted separately. Prepared, studied and void are not scored attempts. Keep answers, feedback and hypothetical scenarios separate from career facts. Update next priorities and handoff.
