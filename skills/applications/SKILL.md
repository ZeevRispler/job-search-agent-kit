# Applications

## Materials


Read current authorization, candidate evidence and unknowns, exact role note, tracker and the resume-source section of profile/CANDIDATE.md. Resolve recruiter/referral overlap before direct application work. Tailor lightly: emphasize, reorder or clarify genuinely relevant existing experience, and shorten less relevant detail where useful. Preserve the candidate’s authentic voice and overall career story. Do not rewrite every bullet to mirror one posting, copy its distinctive wording or keywords, or inflate scope to satisfy a requirement. Keep the general resume when tailoring adds little value, and briefly explain meaningful changes. Match actual evidence to the role rather than copying posting phrases. Keep dates, titles, metrics, scope and personal contribution accurate. Ask or leave unknown required personal answers unresolved.

Preserve originals and create distinct versions. Store artifacts under resumes/tailored/JNNN/ or resumes/materials/JNNN/. Check exported document layout with available rendering tools before delivery. If rendering is unavailable, disclose the verification limit. Keep proposed phrasing separate from verified experience.

Follow the user's current submission boundary and tool rules. Do not infer application/contact authorization from preparation. Leave optional demographic answers unspecified unless directed. Record actual material versions, status evidence and next action in canonical records and CONTEXT.md.

## Status reconciliation


Read tracker and matching role notes first. Match employer aliases, title/requisition, dates, board IDs and URL aliases. Keep ambiguous role linkage unresolved. Use connected mail only within the user's authorized scope, with bounded queries and recorded coverage limits.

Separate discussion, intent to submit, referral request, referral confirmation, submission report, acknowledgment and interview/outcome evidence. Do not infer submission from a prepared form or promised action. Append event date when known, report date, source, role-link confidence, uncertainty and next step. Retain earlier events and exact submitted file when known. Do not guess an unknown general-resume filename.

Update canonical tracker and role note before current handoff. Resolve potential recruiter/direct duplicates before further applications. Reading messages does not authorize replies, labels, calendar changes or outreach.
